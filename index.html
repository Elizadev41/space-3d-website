Mercury

The closest planet to the Sun and the smallest, it has no atmosphere and experiences extreme temperature shifts.

Venus

The hottest planet with a thick, toxic atmosphere; it rotates clockwise and takes longer to rotate once than to orbit the Sun (a day is longer than a year).

Earth

Our home planet, the only one known to support life and featuring surface water. 🌍

Mars

Known as the "Red Planet" due to rusty iron in its soil, it features the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.

Jupiter

The largest planet, a gas giant with over 90 moons and a massive storm called the Great Red Spot.

Saturn

Famous for its stunning, complex rings, it has the most extensive, visible ring system made of ice and rock.

My absolute favorite. There's just something about those rings... 💫

Uranus

An "ice giant" that rotates on its side, giving it unique seasons.

Neptune

The most distant planet, known for its deep blue color and incredibly fast, high-speed winds.
