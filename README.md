# My Space Journey

A personal website exploring the solar system through interactive 3D planets, built as a reflection of my childhood wonder and ongoing curiosity.

**Live Demo:** https://space-3d-website.vercel.app/


## What's This?

Growing up, space has always captivated me,especially Saturn. This site brings together my love for astronomy with facts about each planet in our solar system. Each planet is rendered in 3D and rotates in real-time as you scroll through the journey.



## Features

* **8 Interactive 3D Planets** – Mercury through Neptune, each with realistic textures and rotation
* **Personal Story** – About me section sharing my interests in basketball, crochet, travel, and biomechanics
* **Planet Facts** – Educational descriptions for each celestial body
* **Saturn Spotlight** – Special styling for my favorite planet with a personal note
* **Scroll-Based Experience** – Journey through the solar system as you scroll down



## How to Use

* Scroll through the page to explore each planet in order
* Read the facts about each planet alongside its 3D model
* Watch the planets rotate in real-time
* Discover why Saturn holds a special place in my heart


## Why I Made This

I wanted to recreate the sense of wonder I felt as a kid looking up at the stars. Space felt infinite and magical, and Saturn's rings were something I could never stop thinking about. This project is my way of bringing that feeling back and sharing planet facts that once made me curious about the universe.


## Tech Stack

* **HTML** – Page structure and semantic sections
* **CSS** – Custom styling, layouts, and visual effects
* **Vanilla JavaScript** – Planet initialization and scene management
* **Three.js** – 3D rendering engine for planet models and textures


## What I Learned

* How to create and render 3D objects with Three.js
* Loading and applying textures to sphere geometries
* Managing multiple Three.js scenes on a single page
* Building an immersive scroll-based experience
* Handling responsive canvas rendering
* Working with ResizeObserver for dynamic container sizing
